### Examples

<img src="images/poss_transients.png?raw=true" width="300"/>

[Examining the evidence for glints from orbit in 1950s sky photographs](https://donalhill.github.io/poss-transients/)

An independent re-analysis of the VASCO project's claims about transients on 1950s Palomar Sky Survey plates: fewer inside Earth's shadow, more near nuclear weapons tests, and some lying in straight lines. Every number on the page comes from a reproducible pipeline built on the public catalogue, with chance measured against the data itself rather than counting statistics. [Source code](https://github.com/donalhill/poss-transients)

<img src="images/sargassum_demo.png?raw=true" width="300"/>

[Sargassum Coastal Exposure Forecast — Barbados (Live App)](https://sargassum-demo.onrender.com)

Lagrangian particle model forecasting sargassum washup probability around Barbados. 10,000 virtual rafts advected with RK4 integration through synthetic ocean currents and wind fields. Interactive Dash app with coastal risk heatmap and polar exposure profile. [Source code](https://github.com/donalhill/sargassum-washin-model)

<img src="images/pitch_control.png?raw=true" width="300"/>

[Pitch Control & OBSO Dashboard - Metrica Sample Data (Live App)](https://pitch-control.onrender.com)

Implementation of Spearman's (2018) Off-Ball Scoring Opportunity model. Visualises pitch control decomposition into transition, control, and scoring probability surfaces. [Source code](https://github.com/donalhill/pitch-control)

[xG Model Dashboard - La Liga 2015/16 (Live App)](https://xg-model-statsbomb-gfcq.onrender.com)

Built an Expected Goals (xG) model using StatsBomb open data. XGBoost with isotonic calibration, SHAP explainability, and interactive Dash visualisations. [Source code](https://github.com/donalhill/xg_model_statsbomb)
